    The whiteboard goes back to being a whiteboard.

    Most fleets run on a whiteboard, a handful of spreadsheets, a shared calendar and whatever the ops manager keeps in their head. Some run all of that on top of a system that was supposed to replace it. It works, as long as the one person who knows it all is at their desk. Carrier Companion puts the map, the board, the loads and trips, the drivers and equipment, the customers, documents, rates and invoices in one place.

    Fewer tabs. Fewer spreadsheets. Fewer things only one person knows.

    One platform for the people who run the fleet.

    Dispatch and trip planning

    Assign the day in one pass. Loads into trips, trips to drivers, planned once on one board with a record of every change.

    Live tracking

    Know before it's asked. Where's the truck, when it delivers, whether the POD came in. The answer is on the screen when the customer calls, so the call is short.

    Quote to invoice

    From the quote to the invoice. Rates set ahead of time, truckload or LTL. The POD lands on the load. The invoice goes out from the same record, and driver pay settles from it too.

    Driver app

    One app in the cab. What the driver marks on his phone shows up on your board. Pickups, deliveries, photos, signed PODs.

    Integrations

    The tools you already run, connected. ELDs, fuel cards, tracking, your customers' EDI. Set up your way, all in the platform.

    FAQ.

    What is Carrier Companion? +
    A transportation management system for asset-based fleets, five trucks and up. It's built for the office side: dispatch, office managers, coordinators, planners. The map, the board, the loads and trips, drivers and equipment, customers, documents, rates and invoices, in one place.
    What size fleets does it support? +
    From 5 trucks to 500 trucks. Carrier Companion is built to help you manage growth and simplify operations regardless of size.
    We already run a TMS. Why would we switch? +
    Most fleets that talk to us do. The question is what the office does around it: the spreadsheets, the whiteboard, the report only one person can pull. If that list is long, that's the conversation worth having.
    Is this another AI platform? +
    No. Where it helps, the platform is automated: matching available trucks to unassigned loads and pricing the match from your own rates.
    We run a brokerage too. +
    Each division on its own tool. The brokerage on BrokerWare, the fleet on Carrier Companion, natively integrated where the data has to move. Same team builds both.
    How is it priced? +
    By the trip, not by the seat. Add people to the office without adding to the bill. We'll walk through the numbers on the first call.
    Who built it? +
    3PL Systems, the team behind BrokerWare. They've built transportation software for freight brokers for twenty years. Their customers who also run trucks asked for a version built for the asset side, and helped shape it.
