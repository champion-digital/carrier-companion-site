FAQ.

How does Carrier Companion help me manage pricing? +
You set rates ahead of time, by lane, weight range, equipment and service area, truckload or LTL. After that the rates answer for you: a customer books against them, a spot quote on the phone runs against them, and the load lands on your board already priced. Through open APIs, the same rates can show up inside brokers' own systems.
How do I see what needs to be dispatched today? +
The board. Unassigned loads sit where you can see them, next to the trucks that are free, and the same plan shows as a list, a calendar or a map. The system can also match available trucks to open loads and price the match from your rates, so the morning starts with a proposal instead of a blank week.
Does it rate LTL? +
Yes, natively. Most asset systems rate truckload only. An LTL rating engine used to be something only large carriers with an IT budget could afford. A twenty-truck fleet gets the same engine here.
Will it connect to the ELD we already use? +
That's the idea. ELDs, telematics, mileage tools and your customers' EDI connect from inside the platform, with the help text on the screen. You set it up; nobody waits on a vendor. If the one you run isn't there yet, we build it.
Does it handle invoicing and driver pay? +
Yes. The POD attaches to the load, the invoice goes out from the same record, and driver settlement (per mile, per hour or a percentage of the load) comes off it too. Fuel, detention, lumper fees and lease payments sit alongside, with margin visible at the load level.
Is there a driver app? +
Yes. What the driver marks on his phone shows up on your board: pickups, deliveries, photos, signed PODs.
Can I control who sees what? +
Roles built from granular permissions, and the menu changes with the role. Credentials and endorsements (CDL, hazmat, tanker) block an invalid assignment before it happens. Every change leaves a record.
