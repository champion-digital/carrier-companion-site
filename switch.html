FAQ.

How long does the switch take? +
A fleet moving off spreadsheets is a different job from one leaving a system it has run for fifteen years. The first call is where we figure out which one you are, and you'll get a straight answer on timing before you commit to anything.
What happens to our current data? +
It comes with you. Customers, drivers, equipment, rates, documents and load history move into Carrier Companion, so nothing you've built over the years stays behind in the old system.
Do we have to stop running loads while we switch? +
No. The board you're on today keeps running until the new one is ready, and the cutover happens on a day you pick.
Our customers send EDI. +
Set it up easily, in the interface. 204s in, 214s out, and the rest.
Who do we call when something's wrong? +
A small team, without layers of approval in the way. The people who built the product are the people you talk to.
